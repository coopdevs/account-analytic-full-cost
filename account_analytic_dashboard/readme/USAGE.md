1. Go to **Accounting > Reporting > Monthly Analytics** to review the revenue
   and expenses of the whole plan hierarchy month by month. Expand or collapse
   the branches, change the period with the search filters, or export the table
   to a spreadsheet.
2. Go to **Dashboards > Analytics** for the overview. Use the **Period** and
   **Line** filters at the top, and click on the monthly chart to open the
   Monthly Analytics pivot.

The dashboard is generated by `tools/build_dashboard.py`: to change its layout,
edit the script and run it again to update `data/files/analytic_dashboard.json`.
With `spreadsheet_dashboard_oca` installed, it can also be edited from the user
interface.
