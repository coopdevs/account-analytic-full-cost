This module gives an overview of the analytic accounting of the company,
month by month, with the revenue and the expenses of every analytic
plan.

Analytic lines get two stored measures, **Revenue** (the amount when it
is positive) and **Expenses** (the amount when it is negative, kept
negative), so that revenue and expenses can be shown side by side and
add up to the amount.

It adds:

- **Accounting > Reporting > Monthly Analytics**: a pivot with the whole
  project plan hierarchy down to the analytic accounts in rows, the
  months in columns, and the revenue, expenses and amount as measures,
  with the totals of every level of the hierarchy. It relies on the plan
  levels of ``account_analytic_searchpanel``, so the levels a branch
  does not reach are skipped and the plan rows are shaded by depth.
- An **Analytics** spreadsheet dashboard, in the Finance section of the
  Dashboards app: key figures (revenue, expenses, result and margin),
  revenue and expenses by month, the result by line (first level of the
  project plan), the monthly result by line, and the analytic accounts
  with the highest expenses and revenue. It can be filtered by period
  and by line.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Accounting > Reporting > Monthly Analytics** to review the
   revenue and expenses of the whole plan hierarchy month by month.
   Expand or collapse the branches, change the period with the search
   filters, or export the table to a spreadsheet.
2. Go to **Dashboards > Analytics** for the overview. Use the **Period**
   and **Line** filters at the top, and click on the monthly chart to
   open the Monthly Analytics pivot.

The dashboard is generated by ``tools/build_dashboard.py``: to change
its layout, edit the script and run it again to update
``data/files/analytic_dashboard.json``. With
``spreadsheet_dashboard_oca`` installed, it can also be edited from the
user interface.
